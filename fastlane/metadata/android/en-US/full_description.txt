Mensis is a menstrual cycle tracker that keeps your data on your phone. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The code is open source under the MIT licence.

What you can log
• Period days and flow, from spotting to heavy
• Symptoms, mood, sleep and notes
• Waking temperature, cervical mucus and LH tests, if you want the full sympto-thermal set
• Pregnancy tests, and pregnancy mode with the estimated due date
• Contraception: combined or progestin-only pill, patch, vaginal ring, injection, implant, hormonal or copper IUD
Nothing is ever required: you decide how much the daily log shows.

Ranges, not guesses
• After one complete cycle, Mensis estimates when your next period starts, as a date range with how many cycles it rests on.
• After three cycles it adds an estimated fertile window and shows how much your cycles vary.
• A waking temperature shift marks ovulation after the fact; without temperatures, ovulation is estimated from cycle lengths and labelled as such.
• With a hormonal method, Mensis predicts the withdrawal bleed or pauses predictions, depending on the method.
• Insights: cycle and period lengths, luteal phase, temperature charts and symptoms that come back in the same phase.
• A PDF report for your doctor, created on the phone.

Private by design
• Everything is stored in an encrypted database whose key is protected by the phone's Android Keystore.
• Optional app lock with the phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. Screenshots and the preview in recent apps are blocked by default.
• Reminders are scheduled on the phone. Notifications say only "Reminder" unless you choose other wording.
• The app never contacts a server unless you turn on Google Drive backup.
• Optional backup to your own Google Drive: the data is encrypted on the phone with a password only you know, then stored in Drive's hidden app folder. Google cannot read it, and neither can the developer. There is no password reset.
• Export to JSON or CSV, with optional password encryption. Import from a Mensis export, from drip. or from any CSV file; Clue import is experimental.
• Works without Google Play services; only Drive backup is then unavailable.

Important
Predictions are statistics about your own history. Mensis is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It is not a method of contraception. Please consult a healthcare professional about any question concerning your health.
