# Mensis

**A cycle tracker that keeps your data on your phone.**

Mensis is a private menstrual cycle tracker for Android: no account, no analytics, no ads. Everything you
log stays on the phone in an encrypted database; an optional backup to your own Google Drive is
encrypted on the phone with a password only you know. The code is open source under the MIT licence.

## Join the test

Mensis is in a closed test on Google Play, and every tester counts: Google Play lets a new app go public
only after at least 12 testers have stayed in its test for 14 days in a row. You need an Android phone
(Android 8 or newer) with Google Play, and the same Google account in every step.

**Invited by e-mail?** Your Google account is already on the test, so it takes two steps:

1. **Become a tester:** <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.deutschdreamers.mensis>, then
   *Become a tester*.
2. **Install Mensis:** <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.deutschdreamers.mensis> (this
   page only opens once you are a tester), or use the Google Play link on the page from step 1.

**Not invited?** Join the testers group first: <https://groups.google.com/g/mensis-testers>, then
*Join group*. The group is only the sign-up list: the developer adds its members to the test by hand,
usually within a day, and then the two steps above work. Only the developer can see members' addresses,
and the group sends no messages.

Then please **stay in the test for at least 14 days**, ideally with Mensis installed. Testers who leave
earlier do not count towards the 12.

If the test page says it is not available, check that it uses the Google account you were invited with
or that joined the group (switch accounts with the profile picture); after joining the group, try again
the next day. In the Play Store app, switch to the same account the same way.

Your data stays on your phone: uninstalling Mensis deletes it, so turn on backup or export first.
Problems and ideas go to the [issue tracker](https://github.com/ritwikshanker/mensis/issues); please do
not include real cycle data.

## What it does

- Log period days and flow, symptoms, mood, sleep and notes; waking temperature, cervical mucus and LH
  tests if you want them; pregnancy tests and your contraception. Nothing is required.
- Next period and fertile window as date ranges, with how many cycles they rest on.
- Insights, a PDF report for your doctor, discreet reminders and a pregnancy mode.
- Export and import (JSON, CSV, drip.), and an optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.
